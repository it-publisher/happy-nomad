# 📄 Visas & documents — Argentina

> Source: expat Telegram chat · Updated: 2026-08 · Period: Dec 2025 – Jun 2026 (1,543 msgs in slice)

⚠️ **Verify every claim here against an official source.** Migration rules moved repeatedly across this window.

## Entry

- Russian passport holders: **visa-free**, 90 days. Practically no visa route exists for them — residency is applied for from inside the country. `✅ confirmed`
- Kazakhstan: officially one month per year. `⚠️ single mention`
- 🔴 **Decree 366 (May 2025)** requires on entry: return ticket, itinerary, **medical insurance for the whole stay**, accommodation booking. Enforcement is uneven — several travellers reported only the accommodation booking being checked. `✅ confirmed, enforcement disputed`
- Border officers may ask for a return ticket. Rare in practice, but participants describe a clear tightening trend through 2026. `⚠️ reported perception`

## Status ladder

| Stage | What it is |
|-------|-----------|
| **Tourist** | 90 days, visa-free |
| **Precaria** | A temporary slip issued while a DNI application is processed. **Not residency** |
| **DNI** | The residency decision itself, and the document. This is what counts |
| **Permanent residency** | After 3 years of *temporary residency* — precaria does not count |
| **Citizenship** | After 2 years holding a DNI |

- No language requirement for DNI or for citizenship. `✅ confirmed`
- Grounds for DNI: long-term study (university or *terciaria*), work, rentista, family.
- ⚠️ **Nobody can promise a DNI processing time.** Repeated question, never a firm answer.

## Overstay

Reported as carrying no penalty beyond trouble re-entering later. `⚠️ single mention — do not rely on this`

⚠️ When a precaria expires, Migraciones checks your exit records against its database.

## Costs & procedure

| Item | Price | Date |
|------|-------|------|
| Extension of stay, at the Migraciones office | 40,000 ARS, **card only** | 2026-03 |
| Consulate appointment service (third party) | 70–130 USDT | 2026-03 |

- For the extension, arrive roughly an hour before the usual entry time. `⚠️ single mention`
- 🔴 **March 2026: migration service fees were raised.** Figures above predate or coincide with that — re-check.

## Studying as a route

- CBC at UBA is a full first year of university, not a prep course, and its paperwork counts for Migraciones. `✅ confirmed across several messages`
- For nationals who do need a visa, a student visa must be obtained **before** entry; issued for 3 months against an invitation.
- ⚠️ Participants repeatedly advise **against** applying for a student visa from Russia — reported refusals.

## 🔴 Where the lack of a DNI bites

A CABA decree (roughly March 2025) restricted city public hospitals to DNI holders — **a precaria is not accepted**. `✅ confirmed, discussed repeatedly` → `Healthcare.md`
