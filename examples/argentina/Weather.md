# 🌧 Weather & seasons — Buenos Aires

> Source: expat Telegram chat · Updated: 2026-08 · Period: Dec 2025 – Jun 2026 (211 msgs in slice)

Buenos Aires has no monsoon and no hard winter. The season matters here for one reason, and it is not comfort.

## Calendar

| Period | What happens |
|--------|--------------|
| December – February | Summer, peak tourist season |
| Winter | Mild by northern standards |

## 🔴 What actually matters: rain stops the city

The recurring practical theme, repeated far more than temperature:

- In rain, transport "doesn't work properly" — ride-hailing cars stop appearing
- Fares reach the top of the 20,000–60,000 ARS band
- Waits stretch; a car may not come at all
- Participants describe **missing flights** because of it

➡️ **Book airport transfers in advance.** Do not plan to hail a car on a rainy morning. → `Transport.md`

## Clothing

For winter, one traveller's account: autumn jackets with rain shells over them were enough — but warm trousers and gloves were recommended. `⚠️ single mention`
