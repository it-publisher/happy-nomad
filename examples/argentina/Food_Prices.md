## Where people shop

- **Chains**: Coto, Carrefour, Jumbo, DIA
- **Ferias** and *verdulerías* for produce, *carnicerías* for meat
- **Mercado Libre** for everything else; **Mercado Pago** as the payment layer

## Notes

- Water is delivered in 20-litre bottles to the door, cooler included as a one-off purchase. Roughly two weeks per bottle for one household. `⚠️ single mention`
- *Asado* comes up as the default social occasion rather than as a cost item.
