## 🔴 Access depends on your document, not your money

A CABA decree (roughly March 2025) restricted city public hospitals to **DNI holders**. A **precaria is not accepted**. `✅ confirmed, discussed repeatedly`

This is the single most consequential fact in the slice: people on precaria with no insurance have described being caught between a public system that will not take them and private care they had not budgeted for.

- **Carlos G. Durand** was named as a decent public hospital by someone using it without insurance. `⚠️ single mention`

## Private cover

## Insurance on entry

🔴 Decree 366 requires medical insurance **for the whole stay, through to the return flight date** — not a two-week policy. Enforcement at the border is uneven. `✅ confirmed` → `Visa_Documents.md`

One traveller reported a two-week policy for two people at ~8,000 RUB, bought before flying. `⚠️ single mention — note it does not satisfy the whole-stay requirement`
