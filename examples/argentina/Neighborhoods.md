## The Palermo argument

Both sides are represented, and the split is worth keeping:

**For** — "for a first rental, pick Palermo to find your feet; Belgrano and Recoleta also work, but food there is pricier." `2026-01`

**Against** — "why Palermo. Crowds, rents and restaurant prices ×2, and your bag gets cut in a second precisely because of the crowds." `2026-01`

## Safety, as residents describe it

- ⚠️ **Pickpockets follow tourists.** "Non-premium" barrios are described as *safer* for that reason — Palermo and Recoleta are where bags get lifted. `⚠️ reported perception, not statistics`
- Further from the centre, participants report more homelessness and more theft risk. `⚠️ reported perception`
- Puerto Madero landlords often want the full lease term upfront plus a month's deposit. `⚠️ single mention` → `Housing.md`
