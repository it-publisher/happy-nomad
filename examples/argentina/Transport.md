## 🔴 Rain breaks the transport system

The single most repeated practical warning in this slice:

- In rain, "nothing works properly" — cars stop appearing
- On holidays a car can take 20 minutes; in rain it may simply never arrive
- Fares climb to the top of the range

➡️ **Order an airport transfer in advance rather than hailing on the day.** Participants describe missed flights as a real, if uncommon, outcome. `✅ confirmed across several messages`

## Public transport

- **Subte** (metro) and **colectivos** (buses) — the everyday network, paid with a **SUBE** card
- Long-distance buses are also used for sending cargo between cities `⚠️ single mention`

## Bicycles

Bike parking at a subte entrance is described as noticeably safer than a street post — though quick-release parts are still a concern. `⚠️ single mention`
