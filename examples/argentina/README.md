## What this example is meant to show

- **Every figure carries a date** — and in a high-inflation country that date is the most important part of it
- **Confidence tags are used honestly**: `✅ confirmed` where several people said the same thing, `⚠️ single mention` where one person did, `❓ needs check` where the chat asked and never answered
- **Contradictions are kept as contradictions.** The Palermo argument in `Neighborhoods.md` has both sides, because the chat had both sides
- **Thin topics say so.** `Weather.md` is 211 messages and admits it — an honest gap beats an invented section
- **What the filter removed is stated**, so you can see what the engine decided was an ad rather than a fact

## Accuracy & verification

- **Source**: expat Telegram chat, 22,697 messages, Dec 2025 – Jun 2026
- **Stage 0**: 9,807 topic-matched lines across 11 slices, after de-duplication and spam removal
- **Filtered out**: repeated motorcycle-rental ads, a Spain-residency service, off-topic threads (Chile, Uruguay), a long landlord dispute that matched several topic keywords
- **Built in two passes**: an initial four-topic run in 2026-04, extended to the full topic set in 2026-08 with a country-tuned `topics.json`
- ✅ Currency channels, mobile carriers and the DNI/precaria distinction confirmed across multiple messages
- 🔴 **Peso prices are Dec 2025 – Mar 2026 snapshots.** Against the staleness scale they are already `⚠️ stale` — they are kept to show the format, not to be used
- ⚠️ Migration rules moved repeatedly across the window and continued moving after it. Verify anything visa-related against an official source
